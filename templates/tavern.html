The Tavern

A community application for EldVatten members, built for blogging, events, voting, messaging, and more.

The Tavern also connects with other parts of the site, keeping relevant community information available to those curious about joining.

EldVatten Community

While Eldvatten has had various means of communication in the past and in the present, there has always been a need for a centralized place to connect and share information. So was born The Tavern.

It allows members to stay connected, share updates, and participate in discussions. It sends notifications about events and updates, and is especially useful for communication during events. It provides a space for centralized votes by community members, as well as member and group messaging.
