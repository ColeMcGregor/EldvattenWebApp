About EldVatten

EldVatten is a group of volunteers dedicated to learning about, recreating, and sharing the culture and history of the Viking Age. This often extends into Viking adjacent cultures as well.

Who We Are

EldVatten is the public name of The Birka Era Research and Re-enactment Society, a non-profit educational organization focused on increasing understanding of Viking-era life, culture, technology, seafaring, and exploration.

While we do specialize in the Viking era, we also study the people and cultures the Vikings encountered through travel and trade, as well as any kind of fighting that can be appropriately pieced into the age or kinds of fighting the Vikings engaged in.

Living History

We try to do more than just read about history. EldVatten members prefer to engage with history in a more physical sense, gearing up and learning to fight together.

Historical Fighting

Fighting is the main part of EldVatten for mmost of our members. We train, build and maintain equipment, and participate in several different historical fighting arts. Abridged, this would include shortsword, longsword, greatsword, daggers, axes, polearms, greatshields, and much more. We will often pick up a new weapon if enough interest is shown.

Arts, Crafts & Research

Members are often involved in making and maintaining their own armor and other equipment used for fighting with EldVatten. Whenever possible, weapons are made from rattan to reduce the risk of serious injury if a weapon breaks during use. Some equipment can be bought ready to use, but most members will end up helping make, modify, or maintain something at some point.

Education & Reenactment

EldVatten participates in living history events, historical reenactments, exhibits, and educational demonstrations for people and organizations interested in Viking history. While not mandatory*, participation in many of these is encouraged.

* Some events are required for members to retain their membership status and roles

Learning & Making

People come in all shapes and sizes, and we are here to support you in your journey. We have several members who are experienced in various crafts and can help you learn and improve your skills. This is how many members have acquired their armor, clothing, and weapons.

Much of this is done as a group or at least in pairs, so that mistakes can be caught and corrected. It also ensures correct sizing and function, as hitting eachother can be dangerous.

New to EldVatten?

We love new people to come and join us. We are welcoming of all, and tolerant of all except the intolerant. Leave your disagreements and prejudices at the door. We welcome debate and argument, we have zero tolerance for any form of assault, harassment, or discrimination.

Come to a public event or reach out and we can discuss how you might get involved!
