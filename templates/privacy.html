Privacy

This page explains what information EldVatten may collect through this website and community application, why we keep it, and how it may be used.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

About This Policy

EldVatten operates this website and its associated community application to support communication, events, membership, and the general operation of the group.

We try to collect only the information that is useful for those purposes. This policy describes the kinds of information the site may store and what happens to that information while you use the site.

Information We Collect

The information collected depends on which parts of the site you use. This may include:

  • Your name, display name, or SCA name.
  • Your email address and account information.
  • Profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Community groups, offices, or other EldVatten affiliations.
  • Event RSVPs and attendance information.
  • Posts, comments, messages, and other content you submit.
  • Action items and their completion status.
  • Contact form submissions.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the site.

Not every user will provide or generate every type of information listed above.

How We Use Information

Information stored by the site may be used to operate EldVatten's community tools and help members coordinate with one another.

This includes uses such as managing accounts, displaying appropriate community information, organizing events, delivering notifications, handling member communications, assigning action items, moderating the community, and responding to questions sent through the website.

Information may also be used when reasonably necessary to maintain the security, reliability, and administration of the site.

Public and Private Information

Some information on the EldVatten website is intended to be public. Public events, public posts, and other information specifically marked for public visibility may be viewed without a member account.

Other information is restricted to registered users, EldVatten members, particular community groups, or users with specific administrative permissions.

The site will not intentionally treat member-only content as public content unless its visibility has been set accordingly.

Private Messages

The Tavern may include direct and group messaging between members. These messages are not intended to be publicly visible.

However, messages stored through the EldVatten application are not guaranteed to be private from authorized site administrators. Administrators may review messages when reasonably necessary for moderation, safety, technical support, investigation of reported behavior, or administration of the service.

To be abundandly clear, any and all communications may be reviewed by authorized administrators, between any members, at any time, and there is no guarantee of privacy. The messaginging service should not be assumed to be private, and you are responsible for any and all statements you make to others if you engage in using the service. Any and all crimes or illegal activities conducted through the platform may be reported to the appropriate authorities depending on the disgression of those involved, administrators, and EldVatten leadership. However, unless administration finds evidence of a crime, any and all communications will require a signed judicial warrant to be accessed by law enforcement.

Message read information may also be stored by the application. Normal users will not be shown read receipts unless that feature is specifically added later. Authorized administrators will have access to message read time and participant information when administering the system.

Retention and Archiving

Information may remain stored for as long as it is reasonably useful for operating EldVatten's community systems, maintaining organizational records, resolving disputes, or protecting the integrity of the service.

Community posts and other long-term community records may remain stored until they are removed by a user with appropriate authority.

Older messages may eventually be removed from the active database and placed into administrative archives. Archiving a message does not mean that the message has been permanently destroyed. While archived messages may at some point be destroyed, there is no guarantee of permanence, or again, privacy.

Data that is no longer needed may be removed when practical.

Cookies and Login Sessions

The site may use cookies or similar browser storage when necessary for features such as signing in, maintaining a secure session, protecting forms, and remembering limited site preferences. If you choose to use the site, you are agreeing to the use of these technologies. Any removed cookies will require signing in again. These cookies do not store any personal information not relevant to site functionality.

We do not intend to use the EldVatten community application for advertising or behavioral advertising. This will change only if proposed to the community and voted upon.

Sharing Information

EldVatten does not operate this site for the purpose of selling member information.

Information may be accessible to other users where required by the feature being used. For example, a profile name may appear in the member directory, an RSVP may be visible to an event organizer, or a post may be visible to the audience selected for that post.

Information may also pass through third-party services that are needed to operate the site, such as email delivery or hosting services. This section will be updated as those services are selected and configured.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, through a signed judicial warrant or when reasonably necessary to address misuse or protect the operation of the site.

Your Profile and Contact Information

Members may be given controls over which contact information appears in the member directory.

Some information may still be required internally for account operation even when it is hidden from other members.

If information associated with your account is incorrect, you may contact an EldVatten administrator, or in most cases, update it yourself.

Account Removal and Data Requests

Members may contact EldVatten about correcting information, disabling an account, or requesting removal of information associated with them. Requested information for removal will be inspected and handled according to the applicable policies and legal requirements.

Some information may need to be retained where it is part of an organizational record, moderation record, archived communication, or content involving other members. This is not optional. You consent to this by using this application and site.

Requests will be considered based on the information involved and the reason it is being retained or removed.

Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures will be used to protect information stored by the site and to restrict access according to a user's account and permissions. This includes encryption of sensitive data.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. Members should avoid submitting information through the community application that is not reasonably necessary for its intended use.

While we strive to maintain the highest level of security, no system can be completely immune to risks, and you should hesitate before sending any important information, such as banking, legal, sensitive, or other relevant information that you would not want a complete stranger knowing or having access to across any network or into the site, application, or messaging system.

Eldvatten, the Society for Creative Anachronism, and any and all affiliates and partners, shall not be liable for any damages arising from the use of or inability to use the site or application, unless due to negligence or willful misconduct.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the EldVatten website and community application change.

Significant changes to how member information is collected, used, or retained should be reflected on this page.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy or how information is handled by the EldVatten website, please contact us.
