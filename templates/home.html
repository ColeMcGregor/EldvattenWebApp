About EldVatten

EldVatten is an all-volunteer group dedicated to exploring, recreating, and sharing the culture and combat of the Viking Age through fighting, crafting, learning, and events. This is done mostly through hands-on experience and direct engagement with the subject matter, often via sword.

While the Vikings are at the heart and idea of what we do, our activities also extend past to the cultures they encountered through travel, trade, and conflict, as well as the histories of other peoples and societies that might be relevant to the general ideas and themes discussed, studied, and engaged in.

What We Do

While we can't possibly list everything, here is a glimpse of what we do and how we look doing it:

Fighting

We engage in combat, combat training, and even participate in tournaments. From sword and shield to ballistae, there is something for everyone.

Arts & Sciences

Armor, weapons, and the like are expensive and don't always fit well. Because of this, we tend to build our own and teach others to do the same.

Events & Community

Everything we do is an event of some form, from practice sessions and crafting days to encampments and large-scale tournaments.

Get Involved

With multiple ways to connect and participate in our community, there is a way for just about everyone to get involved.
